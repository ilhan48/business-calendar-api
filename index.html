SECTION 01 // OVERVIEW

Deterministic business day, half-day, and statutory holiday math over HTTP.

Financial settlement, SLA tracking, and ERP invoicing fail when holiday tables and half-day eve sessions are maintained by hand. BizCal computes working-day offsets and date-range breakdowns across fixed holidays, Easter offsets, hijri lunar shifts, and half-day sessions in under a millisecond.
