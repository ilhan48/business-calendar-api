SECTION 01 // OVERVIEW

Deterministic business day and statutory holiday math over HTTP.

Financial settlement, SLA tracking, and ERP invoicing break when holiday calendars are maintained by hand. BizCal computes working-day offsets across fixed holidays, Easter-offset dates, hijri lunar shifts, and weekend substitute rules in under a millisecond.

          SECTION 03 // STATUTORY RULE COVERAGE
        

        

          Supported Jurisdictions & Edge-Case Rules
        

      


      

        

          
            

              Code
              Jurisdiction
              Moveable / Lunar Rules
              Weekend Substitute Policy
            

          
          
            

              TR
              Turkey
              Ramazan Bayrami (3d) & Kurban Bayrami (4d) astronomical tables
              No weekday rollover for fixed civic holidays
            

            

              US
              United States (Federal)
              Nth-weekday rules (MLK, Memorial, Labor, Columbus, Thanksgiving)
              Saturday observed Friday; Sunday observed Monday (5 U.S.C. 6103)
            

            

              UK
              United Kingdom (England & Wales)
              Good Friday, Easter Monday, Early May & Spring/August Bank Holidays
              Royal proclamation substitute Monday/Tuesday when Christmas/Boxing Day hit weekend
            

            

              DE
              Germany (Bundeseinheitlich)
              Computus Easter offsets: Karfreitag (-2d), Ostermontag (+1d), Christi Himmelfahrt (+39d), Pfingstmontag (+50d)
              Statutory holidays falling on weekend are not shifted
            

            

              NL
              Netherlands
              Goede Vrijdag, Tweede Paasdag, Hemelvaartsdag, Tweede Pinksterdag
              Koningsdag (Apr 27) shifts to Saturday Apr 26 when Apr 27 is Sunday
            

          
        

      

    


    
    

      

        

          SECTION 04 // HTTP SPECIFICATION
        

        

          Endpoints
        

      


      

        

          

            POST
            /api/v1/calculate
          

          

            Advances start_date by business_days working days (1–3650), skipping Saturdays, Sundays, and official holidays for country. Returns exact skipped holiday records.
          

        


        

          

            GET
            /api/v1/holidays?country=TR&year=2026
          

          

            Returns the chronological list of statutory holidays for a given country code and calendar year (1970..=2100), including localized names.
